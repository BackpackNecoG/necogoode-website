New Vendor Onboarding Procedure (Rev. 4)

1. Purchasing receives the new vendor request from the requesting department and enters it in the vendor request log.
2. Purchasing emails the vendor our onboarding packet and requests a completed W-9, a certificate of insurance (COI), and a bank letter on bank letterhead.
3. The vendor returns the W-9, COI and bank letter to Purchasing.
4. Purchasing checks that the tax ID on the W-9 matches the vendor's legal name and that the COI meets our minimum coverage.
5. AP verifies the bank letter by calling the bank using a phone number we look up ourselves, and confirms the routing number and account number.
6. Credit runs a credit check on the vendor and sets a credit limit.
7. If expected annual spend is over $25k, Karen (Operations Director) approves too.
8. Treasury sets up the vendor bank account and routing numbers for ACH payment.
9. Operations confirms the vendor can meet our delivery schedule and adds them to the approved supplier list.
10. AP activates the vendor in the system and notifies Purchasing and the requesting department.
