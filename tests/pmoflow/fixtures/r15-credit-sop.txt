Credit Limit Request Procedure (as-is)
1. Sales rep receives a credit limit request from the customer by email and enters the customer name, account number, requested limit and bank account into the request form.
2. Sales rep attaches the latest credit report and sends the request to AR.
3. AR Specialist (Jordan Lee) checks the customer's payment history and pulls the risk score from the credit bureau.
4. If the requested limit is $50,000 or less and the risk score is 60 or below, the request is auto-approved and AR Specialist updates the limit in the ERP. Otherwise, go to step 5.
5. Credit Manager (Priya Nair) reviews the request and recommends approve or reject.
6. If the requested limit is over $250,000, Controller (Sam Wu) must sign off before the limit is changed; otherwise the Credit Manager approves.
7. If rejected, AR Specialist emails the customer the reason and closes the request.
8. AR Specialist updates the credit limit in the ERP and notifies the sales rep and the customer.
