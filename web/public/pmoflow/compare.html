PMOFlow on its own, or alongside what you have.

PMOFlow covers a process change end to end: map, plan, track, test, approve and launch. PMOFlow manages the change to a process, not each run of it. Use it on its own, or connect the tools your teams already work in. No migration project. Connect what you have, or don’t.

What PMOFlow covers end to end

  • Map: today’s process and the future one, with every new, changed and removed step counted.
  • Plan: tasks generated from the change, owned by named people, in dependency order.
  • Track: who is late, what is blocked and when it will finish, in plain language.
  • Test: tests from each step’s rules and real sample data, keeping statistics only.
  • Approve: each department signs off just its part.
  • Launch: a checklist, a named undo owner and an undo plan.

If you already use one of these

Optional. PMOFlow works without any of them.

With Jira or Azure DevOps

Your engineers keep their boards. PMOFlow creates the work items from the process change, keeps their due dates and dependencies, and reads status back, so the PMO sees the whole change, not just the tickets. (Business plan.)

With Planner or Project

Planner and Project schedule the tasks you type in. PMOFlow works out which tasks the process change needs, and carries them through sign-off, testing and launch.

With Smartsheet

Smartsheet is flexible, so every team builds its own tracker. PMOFlow gives every process change the same path: map, plan, sign-off, test, launch.

With Signavio

Signavio models and governs your processes. PMOFlow takes a change to one of them and runs it to launch: the plan, the people, the tests and the undo plan.

ServiceNow: there is no ServiceNow integration yet. PMOFlow syncs with Jira and Azure DevOps today. If your teams work in ServiceNow, PMOFlow still runs the process change from map to launch on its own; tell us on the demo form so we know you use it.

What PMOFlow deliberately doesn’t do

  • Timesheets. Keep them in the tool your finance team already uses.
  • A general PPM report builder. PMOFlow shows a plain status for each project and the portfolio; custom reporting belongs in your BI tools.
  • Running a process each time it happens. A weekly checklist, or each new vendor or case going through the process, belongs in the tool your team already runs that work in.
  • Process mining or simulation. That is the job of an enterprise process suite.
