Pricing questions

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who creates or changes process maps, plans, tests or launch plans. People who only view, comment, record their own task progress or approve their department’s part are free.

Do we need Jira or Azure DevOps?

No. Every plan works on its own, from map to launch. Two-way sync with Jira and Azure DevOps is a Business-plan feature for teams that already use them. No migration project. Connect what you have, or don’t.

What does the Team plan need from IT?

A one-time setup: your Azure admin runs our template in your subscription, so your records live behind an API in your own tenant. Team doesn’t use the relay or any Jira, Azure DevOps or Teams keys; those come with Business. Nothing is installed on people’s computers. The details

What if PMOFlow goes away?

Your records live in your own tenant, so you keep them. You can export at any time: process maps as BPMN 2.0, work items and the portfolio as CSV, and the change history as CSV or JSON. Enterprise customers can also ask for source code escrow. More on this

Is there a monthly option?

Plans are billed annually. If you want to start smaller, the pilot is a fixed fee for 90 days.

What is “planned” on the Enterprise plan?

Items marked Planned are on the roadmap and not available yet. We will tell you the status of each one on the demo call. See the security page for the full list.
