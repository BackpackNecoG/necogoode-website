Six things in one chain

From “this is changing” to “it’s live, and we can undo it.”

  1. 01

    See exactly what changed

    Draw today’s process and the future one, or paste a procedure you already have. Every step is marked new, changed or removed, with what it used to be.

    • One count everywhere: new, changed and removed steps
    • Before-and-after for each changed step
    • Import a written procedure, BPMN or a Visio flowchart
  2. 02

    Know the information each step needs

    Each step lists the information it uses, who owns it and whether you have it yet. Steps that are waiting on information are flagged before anyone starts building.

    • Owner for every piece of information
    • “We have it” or “Not available yet”, at a glance
    • Steps waiting on information listed in one place
  3. 03

    Get the plan from the change

    Every new, changed or removed step becomes tasks with a named person as owner, in the order the work has to happen. Training and “tell the affected teams” tasks are added for you.

    • Owners are people, not roles
    • A task never starts before the one it depends on
    • Late work shows who needs to act, and why
  4. 04

    Department sign-off on just their part

    Accounts Payable approves the payment steps. Compliance approves screening. Nobody signs off a forty-page document, and if a part changes after approval, only that department approves again.

    • Each team sees only the part it owns
    • Approval requests wait until that part’s work is done
    • Separate people record tests and approve them
  5. 05

    Test with real data, safely

    Check a sample file for lengths, blanks and odd characters. PMOFlow keeps the statistics, never the values. Tests are generated from each step’s rules and the sample.

    • Access to real data is for one project, time-boxed and approved by its owner
    • Coverage counts every changed piece of information
    • Launch waits until every test has passed
  6. 06

    Launch with an undo plan

    Launch day has a checklist, a window that skips weekends and holidays, a named person who can undo it, and the undo steps written down before you go.

    • Final checks confirmed by their owners
    • Temporary data access closes automatically at launch
    • A one-press rollback notice to the project team

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need Jira, Azure DevOps or Planner to use PMOFlow?

No. PMOFlow runs the whole change on its own, from map to launch. If you already use Jira or Azure DevOps, you can keep your workflow: work items sync both ways (titles, descriptions and status), while PMOFlow keeps the due dates and dependencies that come from the process change. Sync is part of the Business plan. No migration project. Connect what you have, or don’t.

Where does our data live?

In your own Microsoft tenant. PMOFlow is a display layer: your process and project records sit behind an API that runs in your Azure subscription, and integration keys stay in your Key Vault. Read the security page.

Who needs a paid seat?

Only editors, the people who build maps and plans. Viewers and approvers are free on every plan. Editor seats start at see pricing a month, billed annually. See pricing.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes, two ways. Open the live demo now, with no sign-up. Or run a 90-day pilot on your own process for a fixed fee, credited to your first year.

Is PMOFlow SOC 2 certified?

Not yet. A third-party penetration test is targeted for Q1 2027 and SOC 2 Type I for Q3 2027, followed by Type II. The security page separates what is in place today from what is planned.

Can we bring the process maps we already have?

Yes. Paste a written procedure and it becomes a map, import BPMN, or import a Visio (.vsdx) flowchart. You can also start from a template such as vendor onboarding or invoice approval.

Does it do timesheets, a report builder or process mining?

No, on purpose. Those belong to the project and process suites you may already own. PMOFlow stays focused on taking one process change from map to launch. See how it fits with your tools.

What happens to what I enter in the live demo?

It lives in a private sandbox for 30 days, alongside fictional Northwind data. Please don’t enter real company or personal data in the demo.
